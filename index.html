Publication

PublishedIJRTI, Vol. 11, Issue 11, November 2026

Design of a 1.2 m Tri-copter VTOL UAV

A design study of a 1.2 m tricopter VTOL aircraft with a tapered NACA 4412 wing, two tilting front rotors, one fixed rear rotor and a laser-cut MDF fuselage. We compared the written specification with our Fusion 360 model and ran first-order estimates for aerodynamics, structure, propulsion and centre of gravity. The study found a large mismatch in the front lever arm and proposed four layout changes that give a positive static margin, then closed with a ground-test plan.

Authors: R Karthik Kiran, Darshit A, Sankeerth S, Manish Reddy S, and Mr Uday M (Assistant Professor). Department of Mechanical Engineering, SJB Institute of Technology. International Journal for Research Trends and Innovation, ISSN 2456-3315, pages b801 to b810. Paper ID IJRTI2611206.
